    Added template enhancements

    August 5, 2026

    We’re making it even easier to organize and manage your templates in Labiteers. 🚀

    Our latest update introduces three highly requested features:

    📂 Nested Template Folders Organize your templates with folders inside folders, making it effortless to keep even large template libraries structured.

    🗑️ Delete Folders Clean up your workspace by removing folders you no longer need.

    📊 Template Count on the Dashboard Get an instant overview of how many templates you have right from the dashboard, helping you stay on top of your content at a glance.

    These improvements are all about helping teams work faster, stay organized, and manage their growing template collections with less effort.

    We’re continuously improving Labiteers based on user feedback, and there’s much more to come.
